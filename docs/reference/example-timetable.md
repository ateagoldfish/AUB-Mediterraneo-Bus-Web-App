## What this tells us

- **Direction:** to campus the bus goes Anavargos → Tepak (+10 min) → Campus; back it reverses.
- **A fourth stop, Old Town, on weekends** (location to collect).
- **Two vehicles at peak times:** a coach and the AUB electric bus (08:30 and 16:20 Mon–Thu).
- **Day groups:** Mon–Thu share one pattern, Friday has its own, Sat–Sun share one.
- Usually only the first stop's time is given; later stops are "about X minutes after".
